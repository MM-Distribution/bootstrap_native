Copy a template here to change it for this site: from Bootstrap Native
(sites/all/modules/bootstrap_native/themes/bootstrap_native/templates), from
a module, or from Drupal core (modules/node/node.tpl.php, for one). Clear
the caches after adding one.

Add Bootstrap classes alongside rather than in place of them.
